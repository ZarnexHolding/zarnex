## Build notes
- **This should be pilot page #2, ahead of IFMC.** The client wall is the single
  most persuasive asset available anywhere in the group, and it exercises a
  section (logo grid) that will be reused by IFMC and eventually by Zarnex itself.
- The PDF's text layer is nearly empty (pages 19–47 are full-bleed portfolio
  images with no text). **Project case studies must come from the client** —
  images alone cannot be captioned accurately, and I will not invent project
  names against real brands like Ferrari or NEOM.
- Client logos are raster inside the PDF. **SVG logo files are required** before
  the wall can ship — and so is written confirmation that Zarnex has the right
  to display each mark.
- Its four-colour identity is by far the loudest in the system. The three-uses
  rule applies as normal: magenta only, and only on its own page.
