## What it does — A

- **Solutions (p2):** DOOH display solutions — media facade, transparent mesh screens, LED billboards, indoor screens, wayfinding, integrated lighting & display systems, support & maintenance. Event technologies — bespoke installations, interactive systems, robotics & techno art, custom display solutions, kinetic ceilings & moving elements, immersive experiences, content & application development, AR/VR.
- **Industries served (p3):** event venues & arenas · skyscrapers & smart cities · cultural centers & museums · public spaces & parks · corporate headquarters · hotels & resorts · retail & malls · government & civic buildings.
- **Digital content creation (p6):** concept → design → production → post → activation. *Resolutions: 2K – 21K.*
- **Marketing & communication (p7):** media buying & planning · media selling · traditional & digital campaigns · creative marketing · data & insights.
- **Smart solutions (p8–p16):** digital wayfinders and kiosks · OLED, holographic and AI-assistant displays · solar wayfinding · smart street light poles · taxi-top screens · LED wristbands and ID cards · interactive games and LED floors · LED arches, tunnels, mesh and canopies · container projection · bus-shelter displays.
- **Sculptures (p17–p19) · stage solutions (p20) · kinetic (p21, p24) · projection mapping (p22–p23) · automotive turntables (p25).**
- **DOOH display networks (p26–p29)** — technical edge, verbatim figures: **pixel pitch 1.9 mm to 16 mm** (indoor/outdoor) · **brightness up to 10,000 nits** · **refresh rate >3840 Hz** for flicker-free video · web-based CMS.
- **Display products (p30–p36):** transparent LED media facades · outdoor LED mesh · linear LED-bar facades · ultra-light fabric micro-LED · adhesive transparent LED film · LED billboards · outdoor LED screens.
- **Lighting solutions (p37–p43):** façade lighting design & consultancy · supply & installation · dynamic media façade systems · smart control (DMX, DALI, IoT) · 3D visualisation & light simulation. Fixtures 2,000–30,000 lm, beam 6°–120°, 2700K–6500K + RGBW, IP66+. Bridge and fountain lighting.
- **Sustainability (p45):** low-energy LED, sensor integration, long-life luminaires, LEED and Estidama.

## Transit DOOH — B

LED taxi-top displays for Saudi Arabia's **Green Taxi initiative** (product name
*HalaTransit* in places), in-car screens, geofenced location-based targeting and
a centralized cloud CMS. Taxi-top spec (p10): double-sided, 3G/4G/Wi-Fi,
>5000 cd brightness, IP65, 40 × 100 cm, >10,000 h lifetime.

B's **vision and mission** are about transport advertising — *"To set a new
benchmark in digital out-of-home (DOOH) advertising…"* — so they are **not used
as the company's vision and mission**. A has none. The page shows neither.
