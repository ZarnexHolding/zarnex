## 5. Existing brand visual language (observed in the PDF)

- **Palette:** pure black, pure white, and a single metallic gold gradient used only on the "Z" monogram. No other brand colour. Subsidiary logos each carry their own accent (Lumen = full RGB spectrum, Hikma = violet, Glitch Morph = magenta, Energ/unnamed mark = green, Laniakea = chrome/cyan, RealIntelligence = signal red in imagery).
- **Monogram:** angular gold "Z" with a chamfered, metallic bevel — reads as machined metal, not flat vector.
- **Graphic devices:** heavy solid black bars used as page headers/footers; a thick rule paired with a thin rule; a halftone dot-grid field (top-right, p1); a small forward-arrow glyph (bottom-right, p1).
- **Type:** geometric sans, double-storey `a`, wide tracking on all straplines (letter-spaced almost to the point of dissolution). A green italic serif is used once for the poetic voice (p7).
- **Geometry:** entirely hard-edged. Zero rounded corners anywhere in the document.
- **Bilingual:** Latin and Arabic set side by side on the cover, equal weight.
- **Imagery:** cinematic, high-production CGI/AI renders — mega-event stages, verdant future cities, immersive light rooms, orbital photography, industrial black-and-white.

## 6. Naming-system insight (analysis, not source)

The subsidiary names encode two coherent systems that the client may not have made explicit:

**Cosmic:** Laniakea (the galaxy supercluster containing the Milky Way), Messier (the deep-sky object catalogue), Astroper, Neptune, Lumen (SI unit of luminous flux), Airwaves.
**Neural / cognitive:** Nu-Ron (neuron), Graymatterz (grey matter), RealIntelligence, Hikma (Arabic: *wisdom*).

Zarnex therefore already reads as **a mind operating at cosmic scale** — gravity holding bodies in orbit, signal travelling between them. This is the foundation of the creative direction (see `01-brand-strategy`), and it comes from the company's own naming, not from an imported concept.
