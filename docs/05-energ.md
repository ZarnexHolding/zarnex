The master Zarnex profile (p23) describes Energ broadly — *"the energy and
sustainability division of Zarnex, powering the group"* — with hydrogen R&D, EV
charging and portable event power. **The company's own two documents describe a
narrower, sharper business: a solar EPC specialising in agriculture.**

Nothing in either ENER-G document mentions hydrogen, EV charging, or powering
Zarnex's own events. I have written the site copy from the company's own
documents, which are more recent and more specific. **Flagging the divergence
so the client can confirm which is current** — the master profile may simply be
describing an earlier or broader ambition.

## Services

**Three system types**
1. **Solar Water Pumping** — deep wells, irrigation networks, livestock water supply; reliable delivery in remote and off-grid locations
2. **Farm Solar Power** — irrigation, cooling, lighting, cold storage and electric fencing across farms and greenhouses
3. **Hybrid Solar** — solar PV with grid, battery storage and backup for operations requiring uninterrupted energy

**Core services**
Off-grid solar solutions · Solar well solutions (deep-well pumps with VFD and smart monitoring) · Comprehensive agricultural energy

**Core business areas** (Projects deck)
Solar PV systems (rooftop & ground-mounted) · Battery Energy Storage (BESS) ·
Hybrid energy systems · Agricultural solar · Industrial energy solutions ·
Operation & maintenance

**Engineering & design**
Site assessment (solar resource, energy demand, water consumption profiling, ROI) ·
System design & simulation (load calculations, pump sizing, PV array & battery design) ·
Project management & handover (procurement, installation, commissioning, operator training)

**EPC capability**
Engineering: energy audit · feasibility · load analysis · electrical & structural design · performance modelling
Procurement: PV modules · inverters · battery storage · mounting structures · combiner boxes · protection devices · monitoring
Construction: civil works · foundations · structure & module installation · cable laying · electrical integration · testing & commissioning

## Six-phase delivery
Consultation & Assessment → Engineering & Design → Procurement → Installation → Testing & Commissioning → Operation & Maintenance

## Sectors served
Residential · Commercial · Industrial · Agricultural
Key markets: Agriculture & farms · Water infrastructure · Livestock & government projects

## Proof points
- Up to **80%** reduction in electricity and fuel costs
- **25+ year** system lifespan
- Energy independence for remote, off-grid farms
