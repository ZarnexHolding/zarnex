## 1. The symbol

Two intersecting elliptical orbits tracing a Z, with a four-point star at the
upper-right crossing. Strokes are **variable weight** — calligraphic, tapering
into the intersections — not a uniform outline.

**The gold solid Z on the Rationale slide is NOT the mark.** It is the
derivation diagram: *merging galaxies → Z lettermark*. Mistaking it for the
deliverable would ship the wrong logo. The delivered mark is the orbit form.

### Approved lockups (five, from the lockup slide)

| # | Lockup |
|---|---|
| 1 | Horizontal — bronze symbol + `ZARNEX` in near-black |
| 2 | Symbol only, bronze |
| 3 | Horizontal — all near-black |
| 4 | Stacked — symbol over `ZARNEX`, with letterspaced `HOLDING` beneath |
| 5 | Symbol only, near-black |

No reversed/knockout lockup is shown in the deck. The applications do use the
mark in white on dark photography, so a mono-white version is implied but not
formally specified — **open question**.

The mark is also used as a large-scale background pattern (tiled, low contrast)
and as an outline graphic device over photography.

## 3. Typography

| Role | Face |
|---|---|
| Headline | **Poppins Semibold** |
| Sub text / body | **Poppins Light** |
| Logotype only | **Nebula Bold** |

Poppins is on Google Fonts — no licensing issue.

**Nebula is licensed and we do not have the file.** It is used only for the
`ZARNEX` logotype, so it should be delivered as *vector artwork*, not as a
webfont — that avoids the licence question entirely and is better practice for a
wordmark. Until the vector arrives the logotype is set in a stand-in and must
not be treated as final.

## 4. Messaging

Verbatim from the deck.

**Positioning statement — "From The Region, For The Future":**

> We Are A Middle East–Based Investment Holding Company Building Long-Term,
> Sustainable Value Across Diversified Sectors. Through Disciplined Capital
> Allocation, Active Stewardship, And Principled Governance, We Partner With
> High-Potential Businesses To Scale Responsibly And Create Durable Returns For
> Investors, Communities, And The Region's Future.

**Campaign lines seen in applications:**
- Bringing the future into focus *(set in a circular lockup around the symbol)*
- Scaling what matters
- Build the future with us
- Leading the journey, inspiring the future

Domain shown throughout: **www.zar-nex.com** — consistent with the registrar.

⚠️ This is a **repositioning**, not just a reskin. The deck frames Zarnex as an
*investment holding company* — capital allocation, stewardship, governance,
returns for investors. The current site frames it as a multi-sector *operator*
("Where Ideas Become Industries"). Those are different stories told to different
readers. Whether the site copy moves to match is **not yet decided** — see §6.

## 6. Open questions

These block a complete rollout. None should be guessed at.

1. **Subsidiary accents.** Does the per-company accent system survive, collapse
   to bronze, or get redesigned? The deck does not mention subsidiaries at all.
2. **Does the investor-facing copy replace the site copy,** or is the deck
   paragraph boilerplate for the mockups?
3. **Nebula Bold** — vector artwork for the logotype (preferred), or the font
   file plus a webfont licence.
4. **Master vector of the symbol.** Currently traced from the deck render; a
   real SVG from the designer would be better.
5. **Reversed/white lockup** — implied by the applications, not formally given.
6. The three subsidiary logos already in the build (IFMC, X Live, ENER-G) are
   untouched by this deck and keep their own identities.
